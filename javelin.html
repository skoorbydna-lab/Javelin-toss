Athletics

by Brooky

Olympics: play all four events in a row (100m, long jump, high jump, javelin) and score points, decathlon style.

Javelin: tap to run, hold to raise the javelin, let go to throw. Faster and later is further, but don't cross the line!

100m: tap the instant the gun fires, then tap each time TAP! flashes. Quicker reactions give bigger bursts of speed. Tap too early and it's a false start!

High jump: for each of the 4 run-up steps, hold and let go when the bar hits 100%. Do the same on the take-off power bar, then tap when the shrinking circle turns solid green at the top of the leap to arch over. Miss that tap and the bar comes down. Three tries at each height.

Long jump: hold for exactly one second, then let go to start. Tap fast when TAP! flashes, then press right at the board and hold while the arrow rises. Let go at the perfect angle.
